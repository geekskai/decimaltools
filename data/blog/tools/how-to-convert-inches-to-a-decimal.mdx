To convert fractional inches to decimal inches, divide the numerator (top number) by the denominator (bottom number). For a mixed measurement, add the whole inches: 3/8 inch = 0.375 inch, so 2 3/8 inches = 2.375 inches. The unit stays inches.

Use the [Inches to Decimal Converter](https://decimaltools.com/tools/convert-inches-to-decimal) to check a measurement, or follow the steps and exact conversion chart below.

## Convert a fraction of an inch to a decimal

For a fraction such as 5/16 inch, divide 5 by 16:

$$Decimal = \frac{Numerator}{Denominator}$$

`decimal inches = numerator / denominator`

The calculation is 5 ÷ 16 = 0.3125, so 5/16 inch equals 0.3125 inch exactly. Another example is 1/4 inch: 1 ÷ 4 = 0.25 inch.

The numerator goes first. Dividing 16 by 5 would give the reciprocal, 3.2, which is a different value.

## Convert whole inches plus a fraction

For a positive mixed measurement, keep the whole inches and convert only the fractional part:

$$Total\ (inches) = Whole + \frac{Numerator}{Denominator}$$

`total inches = whole + (numerator / denominator)`

For example, to convert 5 3/16 inches:

1. Separate the whole inches, 5, from the fraction, 3/16.
2. Divide 3 by 16 to get 0.1875 inch.
3. Add 5 + 0.1875 to get 5.1875 inches.

You can check the result by converting the mixed number to an improper fraction: (5 × 16 + 3) / 16 = 83/16 = 5.1875. It also falls between 5 1/8 inches (5.125) and 5 1/4 inches (5.25).

| Mixed measurement (in) | Decimal inches |
| --- | --- |
| 1 1/4 | 1.25 |
| 1 1/2 | 1.5 |
| 1 5/8 | 1.625 |
| 2 3/8 | 2.375 |
| 2 5/8 | 2.625 |
| 4 5/8 | 4.625 |
| 5 3/16 | 5.1875 |

In this measurement context, a hyphenated entry such as `2-3/8` means 2 + 3/8 inches. Use `2 3/8` in the converter if you want to avoid confusing the separator with a subtraction sign.

## How many decimal places should you keep?

Keep the exact value through intermediate calculations, then round the final result to the precision required by the drawing, spreadsheet, or receiving system. Display precision and manufacturing tolerance describe different things: printing more decimal places does not make a measurement more accurate.

For example, 1/64 inch equals 0.015625 inch exactly. Rounding that value changes it by the amounts shown here:

| Decimal places | Rounded value (in) | Absolute rounding difference (in) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 0.016 | 0.000375 |
| 4 | 0.0156 | 0.000025 |
| 6 | 0.015625 | 0 |

Whether a rounding difference is acceptable depends on the project's tolerance and how the value will be used. Follow the specified rounding rule when one is given. Converting a tape-measure reading to six decimal places preserves the numerical fraction; it does not establish six-place measurement accuracy.

A fraction such as 1/3 inch has a repeating decimal, 0.333… inch. Keep the fraction or sufficient intermediate digits until the final rounding step.

## Decimal inches, decimal feet, and millimeters

Decimal inches express a length in inches using a decimal number. Decimal feet express the length in feet. For example, 6 inches equals 6.0 decimal inches or 0.5 decimal feet, because 1 foot contains 12 inches. To convert a decimal-inch value to decimal feet, divide it by 12.

Converting inches to millimeters changes the unit. The international inch equals exactly 25.4 millimeters, as stated in [NIST's engineering metrology reference](https://emtoolbox.nist.gov/Faq/Faq.asp). Therefore, 3/8 inch = 0.375 inch = 0.375 × 25.4 = 9.525 mm. Use the [decimal inches to millimeters converter](https://decimaltools.com/tools/decimal-to-millimeter) when the destination requires mm.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is 5/8 inch as a decimal?

5/8 inch equals 0.625 inch exactly, because 5 ÷ 8 = 0.625. A measurement of 2 5/8 inches therefore equals 2.625 inches.

### Is 3/8 inch the same as 0.38 inch?

3/8 inch equals 0.375 inch exactly. Using round-half-up rounding to two decimal places, 0.375 becomes 0.38 inch. The difference is 0.005 inch. Use the exact value when the receiving system accepts it, and follow the project's rounding rule when rounding is required.

### Can I convert a decimal inch value back to a fraction?

Yes. For example, 0.625 inch = 625/1000 inch = 5/8 inch. The converter also has a **Decimal → Fraction** mode. If a decimal has already been rounded, the original fraction may be lost: 0.38 inch equals 19/50 inch exactly, while 3/8 inch is 0.375 inch. Choosing a nearby ruler fraction introduces a separate rounding step.
